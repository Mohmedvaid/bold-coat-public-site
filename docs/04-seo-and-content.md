# 04 - SEO and Content Templates

| Project | [City] [Service] Project \| Bold Coat Painters | Winnetka Exterior Repaint \| Bold Coat Painters |
| Blog | Post title \| Bold Coat Painters | trim to fit |

Meta descriptions: unique per page, 140 to 160 chars, one concrete specific (surface types, town names, warranty), end with a soft action. Never reuse a description across pages, this is a hard check.

## Keyword targets

- City pages: house painters [city], painters [city] il, exterior painters [city], painting contractors [city]
- Service pages: [service] chicago north shore, plus surface modifiers (cedar siding painting, stucco painting, brick painting, kitchen cabinet painting)
- /pricing: cost to paint a house exterior chicago, interior painting cost chicago suburbs, painting cost calculator
- Blog: long-tail informational (see queue)

## Schema (JSON-LD)

- Sitewide in the base layout: LocalBusiness (subtype HousePainter) with NAP from config, geo, areaServed listing the 11 towns, sameAs socials, aggregateRating ONLY once real values are in config, image, priceRange "$$$".
- Service pages: Service with provider reference and areaServed.
- City pages and any page with an FAQ block: FAQPage.
- Blog posts: Article with datePublished, dateModified, author as the organization.
- Projects: ImageGallery plus BreadcrumbList. Breadcrumbs on all nested templates.
- Never emit schema for claims not in config (rule 6 in CLAUDE.md).

## City page template (the anti-doorway formula)

Every city page must be impossible to mistake for another city's page. Required sections:

1. Hero: unique H1 and 2 to 3 sentence intro referencing the town's actual housing stock (Winnetka: cedar shake, stucco Tudors, landmark district considerations; Glenview: 90s vinyl and Hardie, newer subdivisions). Content lives in the collection body, written per town, never generated by swapping the city name.
2. Services offered strip linking the five service pages.
3. Projects in and near [City]: 2 to 3 ProjectCards filtered by city or nearby. Until real projects exist for a town, show nearest-town projects with honest labels ("Nearby: Wilmette exterior repaint").
4. Painting homes in [City]: 150 to 250 words on surfaces, weather exposure, prep realities, HOA or landmark notes where true.
5. City-specific FAQ: 3 or 4 questions, at least two unique to the town, FAQPage schema.
6. Reviews filtered to that town when available.
7. Compact estimate form + calculator link.
8. Nearby areas links (3 or 4 sibling city pages) for internal linking.

Minimum 500 words of town-specific prose. If the unique content is not available for a town at build time, the page does not ship in the sitemap until it is.

## Project page template

Cover image, town + services eyebrow, title, scope summary, stats row (duration, surfaces, colors used with brand and name), 8 to 15 photo gallery with before/after slider where pairs exist, 150 to 300 word narrative, links to the city page and service pages, CTA. No street addresses, no full customer names.

## Blog

Hub: featured latest post large, then card grid with category filter (Cost, Exteriors, Interiors, Colors, Projects). No sidebar.

Post template: full-bleed cover, Fraunces headline, byline "Bold Coat Painters Team" with date and reading time, sticky table of contents on desktop for posts over 1,200 words, big inline images, one mid-post CTA card, FAQ block with schema when relevant, related posts, Article schema.

## Internal linking rules

Home links every service and 4 featured cities. Services link all 11 cities in a footer strip. Cities link services, 2 to 3 projects, and 3 to 4 nearby cities. Projects link their city and services. Blog posts link at least one service or city page and /pricing where costs are mentioned. No orphan pages, verified at build with a link-check script.

## Pre-launch checklist

- Unique title, description, H1 on every page (scripted check)
- Schema validates in Rich Results test for one page per template
- Sitemap excludes /thank-you and any city page lacking unique content
- robots.txt allows all, references sitemap
- 301 map verified against the old WP sitemap
- All images have alts, hero images have explicit dimensions
- Lighthouse mobile 95+ on Home, one service, one city, /pricing
