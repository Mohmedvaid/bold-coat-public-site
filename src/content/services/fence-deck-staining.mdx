Decks and fences on the North Shore see snow, sun, and standing moisture. Gray boards and peeling film finishes are common when a previous coat was applied over dirty wood.

We clean the surface, remove what will not hold, and apply stain or seal suited to the species and exposure. Horizontal deck boards and vertical fence pickets get the attention each needs.

If the structure is sound, staining restores color and protection without replacing every board. If rot has taken hold, we will flag it before we open a can.
