Cabinet painting is a craft job, not a weekend roller project. Grease, latex over oil, and soft wood grain all show if prep is skipped.

We remove doors and drawers when the scope calls for it, clean and sand thoroughly, and apply a finish system built for hard use. Edges stay crisp, color stays even, and hardware goes back on square.

If your North Shore kitchen or bath needs a new look without a gut remodel, cabinet painting is often the cleanest path. We will tell you honestly when replacement makes more sense.
