Interior work should feel calm while it is happening and finished when we leave. That means masking that holds, cuts that stay sharp, and coats that cover without telegraphing old repairs.

We walk the rooms with you, note repairs and sheen changes, and put the scope in writing before paint day. Prep includes filling, sanding, and priming where needed so the final coat reads as one surface.

From a single room refresh to a whole-home repaint, the standard is the same: protected floors, clean edges, and a walkthrough you sign off before we pack the van.
