North Shore exteriors take a beating from wind off the lake, long winters, and summer humidity. A coat that looks fine in May can peel by the next spring if the prep was rushed.

We start with a walkthrough that names the surfaces, failed coatings, and trim details in writing. Then we protect the property, remove what will not hold paint, and prime where the substrate needs it. Finish coats go on only after the surface is ready.

Whether the home is cedar in Winnetka, brick in Wilmette, or Hardie in Glenview, the goal is the same: even color, crisp lines, and a finish that lasts longer than a franchise one-day paint job.
