North Shore exteriors usually look best when color supports the architecture instead of fighting it. Cedar, brick, stucco, and Hardie each read differently in lake light.

## Start with the house, not the trend

Soft neutrals, deep greens, and classic trim whites remain common because they fit shake, brick, and limestone accents. Bold front-door colors can work. Full-elevation experiments are harder to live with and harder to reverse.

## Confirm samples on the real wall

Chips lie indoors. Brush outs on the south wall and a shaded elevation before you commit. HOA or landmark-adjacent blocks may need extra care. We confirm palette choices during the estimate so paint day is not a surprise.
