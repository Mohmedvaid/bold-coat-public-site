Cedar looks right on North Shore blocks from Winnetka through Glencoe, but it is not a low-maintenance canvas. Shake and clapboard move with humidity. Failed film traps moisture. Gray fibers will not hold a thin cosmetic coat.

## What lake weather does to cedar

Wind-driven rain, freeze cycles, and sun on south and west elevations open joints and lift old coatings. A crew that washes and rolls the same day often leaves moisture behind. The finish may look fine for a season and then peel in sheets.

## Prep that actually holds

Clean the surface, remove failed coatings, treat bare wood, and seal open joints before finish coats. Name dormers, porch undersides, and window casings in the written scope. Those details fail first when ignored.
