The best predictor of a calm painting project is not a glossy brochure. It is a written scope that names surfaces, prep, products, and daily habits.

## Surfaces and prep

List elevations or rooms, substrate notes, and whether carpentry is included or excluded. If failed coatings are likely, say so before the crew arrives.

## Protection and schedule

Plantings, floors, furniture, and cure time belong in the plan. Occupied homes and commercial spaces need sequencing. A start date without a realistic finish window is incomplete.

## How to compare

Put scopes side by side. Ignore verbal promises that never appear on paper. Review sample [projects](/projects), then request your own estimate through [contact](/contact) when you want that standard applied to your house.
