Most North Shore interior projects happen in lived-in houses. Families work from home, kids need bedrooms at night, and nobody wants a week of chaos for a color change.

## Sequence the house on purpose

Common areas first or private rooms first depends on how you live. Put the plan in writing before paint day. Protect floors from the start, not after the first spill.

## Respect the substrate

Older plaster, updated drywall, and dense trim profiles need different prep. Fill defects that will telegraph through light colors. Degloss trim before finish coats. If a painter treats every wall like new drywall, ask for a clearer scope.

## Keep dust and odor manageable

Mask vents, cover furniture, and choose products suited to occupied homes when the scope allows. Daily cleanup is part of the job, not a courtesy.
