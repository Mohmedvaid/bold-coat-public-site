Northbrook's residential pattern is familiar across much of the northwest suburbs, with its own local mix. Larger lots give homes breathing room. Housing stock spans 1970s and 1980s builds, 1990s updates, and newer construction. Vinyl and Hardie (fiber cement) siding show up often. That does not make painting simple. Trim, fascia, garage doors, and entry surrounds still weather. Prior coatings still fail at joints. Sun elevations still chalk. We treat Northbrook exteriors as real systems, not as "just vinyl" jobs that need a quick roll.

Homes from the 1970s through the 1990s often carry a blend of materials. Wood trim may sit against vinyl fields. Softit and fascia boards may be original while siding was replaced. Paint history can be uneven. We inspect for peeling at windows, open caulk, and weathered wood that will telegraph through a new coat. Prep is matched to what we find. Skipping scraping and priming on trim while coating the field is how projects look unfinished in close light.

Newer Northbrook construction brings Hardie and modern trim profiles. Factory finishes are not permanent. UV, driveway splash, and seasonal moisture still work on every elevation. Spot failures at corners and around penetrations appear before the whole wall looks tired. We photograph those areas during the estimate and write prep steps that fit fiber cement and adjacent wood or composite trim. One product for everything is not a plan.

Larger lots help with ladder placement and material staging, and they still require daily care. Plantings, lawns, and walks need protection. Overspray and chips are not less visible because the property is bigger. We leave the site tidy each evening and keep staging organized. Northbrook owners expect a professional presence for the length of the project.

Interiors in this stock range from older layouts with more wood trim to open newer plans. We protect floors, control dust, and sequence rooms so the house stays usable. Cabinet painting is common in kitchens that have been updated while hallways and living areas still need a coherent color plan. Clear written scopes keep sheen and color consistent across those spaces.

Color choices in Northbrook often favor practical neutrals and classic whites that fit both older and newer homes. We can match existing paint or work from a chip. Weather still rules cure times. Humidity and overnight lows matter on vinyl and Hardie projects just as they do on wood. We schedule around conditions rather than promising brochure speed.
