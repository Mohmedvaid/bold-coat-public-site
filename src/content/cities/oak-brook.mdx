We photograph those areas during the estimate and match prep to the materials on the house. Skipping prep because a home feels new is how early peel happens.

Commercial-adjacent residential pockets can see more road dust and splash than quieter interior streets. Lower siding and garage returns take abuse. One clear scope keeps color and sheen coherent across the project.
